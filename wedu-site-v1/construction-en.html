HOW IT WORKS

Training — not employment

The program is a structured technical training. It is not employment, a job offer or paid work, and it follows fixed supervision rules.

The trainee is never given independent operational authority.

They do not join the site's regular workforce.

Partner sites act as training locations, not employers.

Any subsistence support is fixed and never tied to output.

Every progression requires a written and practical evaluation approved by the instructor.

The final outcome is an implementation plan for your home country.
