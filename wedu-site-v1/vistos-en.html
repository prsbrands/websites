THE VISA

The H-3 training visa

The H-3 is the U.S. visa for those coming to receive structured professional training that isn't available in their home country. It's the legal basis for every W Education program.

The holder can bring a spouse and children as dependents (H-4 visa), accompanying them for the full duration of the program approved by immigration authorities.

Eligibility — requirements

·Minimum age of 18.
·Interest in working or developing in the chosen field.
·Prior experience (not required) or interest in a career change / ministerial calling.
·Financial capacity to support yourself during the program.
·Interest in learning and working within American market standards.
DOCUMENTATION

Required documents

From the applicant

01Scanned copy of a valid passport (at least 6 months before expiration).
02Diploma or certificate (for applicants with experience) or a résumé of your journey.
03Passport-style photo.
04Proof of residence.
05Address where you intend to live in the U.S. (if still abroad).

If you have dependents

·Scanned copy of a valid passport for every dependent (at least 6 months before expiration).
·Proof of family relationship with notarized translation — marriage certificate (spouse) and birth certificate (children).
